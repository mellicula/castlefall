How to play

Castlefall (Castle of the Devil + Spyfall) is a party word game for about six to ten people. An even number is best. It’s playable with a few more, somewhat playable with four or five, and playable in some theoretical sense with three.

It was invented in 2017 by some folks at Epsilon Theta, an independent living group at MIT. Everyone uses the app for words, but the talking happens out loud, in person.

Setup

  1. Everyone joins the same room.

    Share the room link (tap the room name at the top of the game). There’s no list of rooms, so you need the link or the room’s name.

  2. Someone starts a round.

    Everyone sees the same list of about 18 words, shuffled differently on each device. Each player also gets one of those words as their word. Players are secretly split into two teams of equal size (or nearly equal): everyone on a team has the same word, and the two teams have different words.

  3. Talk.

    Figure out who is on your team, or what the other team’s word is, without giving your own word away. Don’t look at anyone else’s screen. If someone asks you to repeat something, it’s polite to do so, and polite to avoid excessive cryptography.

Declaring victory

Talking continues until somebody declares victory, usually by clapping loudly, since the first declaration takes priority. There are two ways to declare:

1 · Name your team

Name N players, yourself included, and claim they are all on your team. Nobody else can declare this way after you.

Start the one-minute timer in the app. Keep talking. If nobody guesses a word (method 2) before time runs out, your team wins if and only if you were right.

While the timer runs, it’s polite to repeat the clues that led to the declaration, so the win comes from good clues and not from overhearing something others missed.

2 · Guess their word

Say what you think the other team’s word is. The round ends immediately, even while a method 1 timer is running. Your team wins if and only if the guess is right.

Anyone can do this during a method 1 timer, including the people who were named and even the person who declared.

Suggested N

6 or fewerN = the size of your team, so you must name your team exactly. With an odd number of players, you also have to guess whether you’re on the smaller or larger team.
7 playersN = 3 for an easier game, or the size of your team for a harder one.
8 playersN = 3
9 playersN = 4, or name 5 people including yourself, of whom 4 must be on your team.
10 playersN = 4
No matter who declared, which method they used, or whether it worked, you win or lose together with your team: the people who had the same word as you.

The app doesn’t track declarations or scores. Everyone’s words are revealed when the next round starts, and players work out who won themselves. Most groups just play round after round until they’re tired.
